# Threat Model and Application Plan

{: .summary }
> **In short:** before you start properly building your application, you need to make an application plan and a threat model. The application plan outlines how you will build your application, and the threat model identifies the most likely attack vectors against it.

---

## Components

### 1. Application plan (1-2 pages)
Outline how you plan to build your application: what methods, tools, and libraries do you plan to use? What components are you still unsure about? Are there different varieties you might test?

It is not a problem if components are not yet set in stone, but you need to have a clear plan for what you will build, and how you will build it. This is essential before you can make a threat model, because it is specific to your design.

### 2. Data-flow diagram (max 1 page)
After you made your plan, make a DFD, as we did before in the course.

### 3. STRIDE over the DFD (max 1 page)
Go over your DFD, and for each part, think about how it could be attacked using the STRIDE categories. Note that more is not always better, really think about what is realistic and what is not, and what are the most likely attack vectors.
